Estimate Follow-Up Checklist for Home-Service Businesses

An estimate can be completed correctly and still go quiet if there is no defined follow-up cadence. Use this checklist to review the process.

What to check

  • Which estimates are still open
  • When each estimate was last contacted
  • Who owns the next follow-up
  • How follow-ups are recorded

Simple next steps

  1. Identify where the lead is currently being lost.
  2. Define who owns the next response.
  3. Set a consistent follow-up process.
  4. Measure whether enquiries reach a completed conversation or estimate.
