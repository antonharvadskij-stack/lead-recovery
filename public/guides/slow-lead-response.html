Slow Lead Response: A Practical Checklist

When response speed varies from lead to lead, some enquiries can go cold. Map the process from the first enquiry to the first useful response.

What to check

  • How new enquiries arrive
  • Who receives them
  • How quickly the first response happens
  • What happens when the first contact fails

Simple next steps

  1. Identify where the lead is currently being lost.
  2. Define who owns the next response.
  3. Set a consistent follow-up process.
  4. Measure whether enquiries reach a completed conversation or estimate.
