How to Stop Losing Leads After a Missed Call

A missed call does not have to become a lost opportunity. The key is having a defined response path that does not depend on someone remembering to follow up later.

What to check

  • Missed calls are identified quickly
  • A response can be sent consistently
  • The enquiry is assigned to someone
  • The conversation is tracked until resolved

Simple next steps

  1. Identify where the lead is currently being lost.
  2. Define who owns the next response.
  3. Set a consistent follow-up process.
  4. Measure whether enquiries reach a completed conversation or estimate.
